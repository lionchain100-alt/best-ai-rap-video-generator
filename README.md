## Photo-to-Rap checklist

1. Use photos you are authorized to process, with clear faces and no unrelated private information.
2. Start with one everyday idea, such as a coffee order, pet entrance or friendly introduction.
3. Read and edit every line. Use original or licensed lyrics and obtain consent from people represented.
4. Review the actual settings and credit quote before submitting.
5. Watch the delivered clip through the final line and download it within its retention window.
